DATA COVERAGE

How complete is this customer picture?

Definitions and limits

Buyer identities use a server-secret HMAC of marketplace, store and a usable buyer username, with Customer Code as fallback. Masked or missing identities are excluded. The same person may appear separately across stores or platforms.

Repeat rate = identified buyers with two or more selected orders ÷ identified buyers. Buyer and persona figures describe the loaded period and selected sales basis, not lifetime acquisition or retention.

Order value = Price × Quantity. Canceled and returned orders are included only with All order value. No payout, refund, fees or profit adjustments are applied. Warehouse-processing and excluded transfer rows remain excluded.

Older snapshots without customer fields need the richer raw export reimported after the backend update. Data is calculated from imported snapshots, not a live marketplace connection. Customer names, phone numbers, full addresses and buyer hashes are never returned by this page’s API.
