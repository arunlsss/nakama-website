About these reports

Shopee revenue uses Sales Amount; Direct ROAS uses Direct Sales Amount. TikTok GMV Max uses gross revenue and SKU orders. GMV Max revenue includes paid and organic orders, so its ROI has a different attribution basis from Shopee ROAS. Counts also differ: Shopee conversions and TikTok SKU orders. These reports are separate from Nakama order GMV, payouts and profit. Ratios are recalculated from matching revenue, spend and counts. Unsupported direct metrics are unavailable.

Upload one Shopee file per day, individually or up to 40 files at once. Use Today, Yesterday, Last 7 days or Custom for a specific date. Dates use Bangkok time; Last 7 days includes today. TikTok daily rows work with the same filters. Missing days remain missing. Re-uploading unchanged dates is ignored; revised values replace those dates. Daily rows can coexist with a containing period total. The total is used for its full period until every day for that store is uploaded, then daily rows take priority. Spend is counted once. Two overlapping period totals for the same store and platform are blocked.
