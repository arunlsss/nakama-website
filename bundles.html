How to read the evidence

A → B is the share of orders containing SKU A that also contain SKU B. B → A uses SKU B as the starting point. All-order share uses every analyzed basket. Relationship (lift) compares observed co-purchases with independent purchasing: 1× is the baseline, above 1× is a positive association.

These are order-level signals, not unique customer counts or guaranteed sales uplift. Small samples and promotions can distort relationships. Store and marketplace filters control the population. “All statuses” includes canceled orders and should be used for investigation, rather than launch decisions.
